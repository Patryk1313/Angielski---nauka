3. Grammar - Past Simple i Present Perfect

Past Simple:
- zakonczone wydarzenie w konkretnym momencie przeszlosci = In 2002, Mercedes suffered a fall.
- czesto z okresleniami czasu zakonczonego = yesterday, last year, in 2010, six months ago

Present Perfect:
- przeszlosc polaczona z terazniejszoscia = The company has recently changed its strategy.
- okres trwajacy do teraz = Since 2005, Mercedes has improved a lot.
- doswiadczenia zyciowe i zawodowe = She has worked for several firms.

Typowe wyrazenia:
- Past Simple = yesterday, last year, in 2010, six months ago
- Present Perfect = recently, since 2009, yet, ever
- oba moga laczyc sie z this week zaleznie od kontekstu

Szybkie rozroznienie:
- Past Simple = kiedy wiemy, kiedy cos sie wydarzylo
- Present Perfect = wazny jest skutek teraz albo okres trwa do teraz

4. Useful language - managing meetings

Starting:
- OK, let's get down to business.
- Right, can we start, please?

Setting objectives:
- The purpose of this meeting is ...
- The aims of this meeting are ...

Asking for reactions:
- How do you feel about ... ?
- What do you think?

Dealing with interruptions:
- Could you let him finish, please?
- Could you just hang on a moment, please?

Keeping to the point:
- I'm not sure that's relevant.
- Perhaps we could get back to the point.

Slowing down:
- Hold on, we need to look at this in more detail.
- I think we should discuss this a bit more.

Speeding up:
- I think we should move on now.
- Can we come back to that?

Summarising:
- OK, let's go over what we've agreed.
- Right, to sum up then ...
