2. Language review - describing trends

Verbs of change:
- rise = wzrosnac
- increase = zwiekszyc sie / wzrosnac
- grow = rosnac
- gain = zyskac / wzrosnac
- improve = poprawic sie
- recover = odbic sie / odzyskac poziom
- fall = spasc
- drop = spasc
- decline = spadac
- decrease = zmniejszyc sie
- level off = wyrownac sie / ustabilizowac
- fluctuate = wahac sie
- peak = osiagnac szczyt

Silny ruch:
- rocket = gwaltownie wzrosnac
- soar = mocno wzrosnac
- plummet = gwaltownie spasc

Zmiana o wielokrotnosc:
- halve = zmniejszyc o polowe
- double = podwoic
- triple = potroic
- quadruple = zwiekszyc czterokrotnie
- increase tenfold = zwiekszyc dziesieciokrotnie

Prepositions do trendow:
- rise from ... to ...
- increase by ...
- stand at ...
- reach a peak of ...
- reach a low point of ...
- a decrease of ...

Przyklady:
- Profits rose by 5% to $1.4 billion.
- Sales have gone up from $19.6 billion to $27.4 billion.
- Orders have levelled off.
- The company recovered after a difficult year.

4. Grammar - modal verbs

Advice:
- should = powinienes / warto
- shouldn't = nie powinienes
- must / mustn't = bardzo mocna rada albo zakaz

Obligation / necessity:
- must = musiec, gdy nacisk pochodzi od mowiacego
- have to = musiec, gdy obowiazek wynika z prawa, zasad lub sytuacji
- mustn't = nie wolno

Lack of obligation:
- don't have to = nie trzeba

Najwazniejsza roznica:
- mustn't = nie wolno
- don't have to = nie musisz, ale mozesz

Przyklady:
- You should do some research before visiting a new country.
- Staff must read the health and safety notices.
- You have to get a visa to enter the country.
- You mustn't smoke inside the building.
- You don't have to pay immediately.

Mini notatka do nauczenia:
- debt = dlug
- dividend = dywidenda
- bear market = bessa / rynek spadkowy
- rise by 10% = wzrosnac o 10%
- rise from 5 to 7 = wzrosnac z 5 do 7
- level off = ustabilizowac sie
- You mustn't ... = Nie wolno ...
- You don't have to ... = Nie musisz ...
