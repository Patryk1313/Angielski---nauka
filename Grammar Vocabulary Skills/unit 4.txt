3. Grammar - noun combinations

Typy noun combinations z p.39 i p.147:
- 's possessive: the company's logo = logo firmy
- noun + noun: business card = wizytowka
- of phrase: director of operations = dyrektor operacyjny
- compound noun: workforce = sila robocza / zaloga

Przyklady z unit 4:
- Internet company = firma internetowa
- culture of autonomy = kultura autonomii
- Google's objective = cel Google
- teamwork = praca zespolowa
- office decorating = dekorowanie biura
- university programmes manager = menedzer programow uniwersyteckich

Zasady do zapamietania:
- pierwszy rzeczownik czesto dziala jak przymiotnik: job description, travel agency
- przy bardziej abstrakcyjnych ideach czesto uzywamy of: cost of living
- niektore compound nouns sa pisane razem: database, headquarters
- przy wyrazeniach miary pierwszy rzeczownik jest w liczbie pojedynczej: a 10-year plan, a 20-minute presentation

4. Skills - socialising, introductions, networking

Greetings:
- Hello, ...
- Hi, ... How are you?
- Great to see you again.
- How's everything going?

Responding:
- Fine, thanks.
- Not too bad, thanks.
- Pleased to meet you.

Introducing yourself:
- I'm from ...
- I work for ...
- I'm in sales.
- I'm in charge of ...
- I'm responsible for ...
- I work with ...

Introducing someone else:
- Can I introduce you to ... ?
- Robert, have you met Vladimir?

Talking about your company:
- The company was founded in ...
- We make / manufacture / sell / distribute / supply ...
- We have subsidiaries / factories / branches / outlets in ...
- We have a workforce of 2,000.

Networking:
- We're very interested in ...
- Do you know anyone who could help us?
- Could you let me have their contact details?
- Could I call him and mention your name?
- Let me give you my business card.

Common interests:
- You and Tom have something in common.
- You both like / enjoy / are interested in ...

Mini notatka do nauczenia:
- subsidiary = spolka zalezna
- headquarters = centrala
- warehouse = magazyn
- customer service = obsluga klienta
- business card = wizytowka
- job description = opis stanowiska
- Can I introduce you to ... ? = Czy moge cie przedstawic ... ?
- Let me give you my business card. = Prosze, oto moja wizytowka.
