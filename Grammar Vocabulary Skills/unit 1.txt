3. Grammar - Present Simple i Present Continuous

Present Simple - kiedy uzywamy:
- fakty i stale informacje = Dior makes luxury fashion.
- rutyny i nawyki = He travels every week.
- rzeczy ogolnie prawdziwe = Many consumers prefer famous brands.
- rozklady i harmonogramy = We launch the new range on Monday.

Typowe okreslenia czasu dla Present Simple:
- usually
- often
- every day
- once a month
- nowadays
- these days

Present Continuous - kiedy uzywamy:
- dzieje sie teraz = The company is working on a new campaign.
- sytuacja tymczasowa = Dior is currently looking for a manager.
- projekt w toku = We are developing a new logo.
- ustalone plany prywatne i zawodowe = I am meeting the client next week.

Typowe okreslenia czasu dla Present Continuous:
- at the moment
- now
- currently
- this year

Szybkie rozroznienie:
- Present Simple = co firma robi normalnie / zwykle
- Present Continuous = co dzieje sie teraz albo tymczasowo

4. Useful language - taking part in meetings

Asking for opinions:
- How do you feel about that?
- What do you think?
- What's your view?

Giving opinions:
- I think that's a good idea.
- I don't think that's a good idea.
- In my opinion, we need new products.
- I think so, too.

Agreeing:
- That's true.
- I agree.
- Absolutely.
- Exactly.

Disagreeing:
- I see what you mean, but I think there's a problem.
- I'm not so sure.
- Maybe, but that's not enough.

Making suggestions:
- How about ice hockey?
- Why don't we talk to the big stores?
- Maybe / Perhaps we could offer incentives.
- I think we should reduce our prices.

Mini notatka do nauczenia:

- product placement = lokowanie produktu

- What do you think? = Co o tym myslisz?
