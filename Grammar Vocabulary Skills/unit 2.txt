1. Starting up - travel vocabulary

Rzeczy, ktore denerwuja ludzi podczas lotu:
- leg room = miejsce na nogi
- lost luggage = zagubiony bagaz
- delayed luggage = opozniony bagaz
- long queues at check-in = dlugie kolejki przy odprawie
- poor-quality food and drink = slabe jedzenie i napoje
- no baggage trolleys available = brak wozkow bagazowych
- overbooking of seats = overbooking miejsc
- flight delays and cancellations = opoznienia i odwolywanie lotow
- jet lag = zmeczenie po zmianie stref czasowych
- delays for security checks = opoznienia przy kontroli bezpieczenstwa
- oversized hand luggage in the cabin = zbyt duzy bagaz podreczny w kabinie

2. Vocabulary - British and American English

Najwazniejsze pary z p.15:
- subway = underground = metro
- city centre = downtown = centrum miasta
- carry-on baggage = hand luggage = bagaz podreczny
- one way = single = bilet w jedna strone
- return = round trip = bilet w obie strony
- freeway = motorway = autostrada
- rest room = public toilet = toaleta publiczna
- elevator = lift = winda
- coach class = economy class = klasa ekonomiczna
- timetable = schedule = rozklad jazdy
- car park = parking lot = parking
- queue = line = kolejka
- check = bill = rachunek
- reservation = booking = rezerwacja
- ground floor = first floor = parter / pierwsze pietro w USA

Uwaga do egzaminu:
- British English i American English czesto maja inne slowo na to samo.
- ground floor w brytyjskim to parter, a first floor w amerykanskim tez oznacza parter + jedno pietro wyzej liczenie jest inne.

3. Grammar - p.18 + p.146: will, going to, Present Continuous, Present Simple

To jest wlasnie ten fragment z Twojej rozpiski: 18 + 146.
Uwaga:
- will i going to sa opisane tutaj jako future forms
- Present Continuous i Present Simple sa tutaj pokazane w funkcji mowienia o przyszlosci, czyli arrangements i timetables
- pelne, ogolne uzycie Present Simple i Present Continuous masz dodatkowo w unit 1 z p.10 + p.146

Going to:
- zamiary i plany podjete wczesniej = We are going to meet our agent.
- przewidywania oparte na planie lub sytuacji = Some airlines are going to increase charges.

Will:
- spontaniczna decyzja w chwili mowienia = I'll call the travel agent.
- obietnica / oferta pomocy = I'll check the back seat of the car.

Present Continuous:
- ustalone aranżacje = I'm travelling to Europe in September.

Present Simple:
- rozklady i programy = The flight leaves at 11:30.

Szybkie rozroznienie:
- going to = juz to zaplanowalem
- will = decyduje teraz
- Present Continuous = mam juz umowiony konkretny termin
- Present Simple = tak jest w rozkladzie / harmonogramie

4. Skills - telephoning and making arrangements

Answering the phone:
- Hello, Carla Rodriguez speaking.
- Good morning, Tiger Ltd.

Making contact:
- I'd like to speak to Martin Krause, please.
- Could I have the sales department, please?

Identifying yourself:
- This is Karin Nordby.
- Karin Nordby speaking.

Stating your purpose:
- I'm calling about ...
- The reason I'm calling is ...

Making arrangements:
- Could we meet on Monday at 11:00?
- How about June 12th?
- Is 9:30 convenient?

Responding:
- That's fine.
- Sorry, I can't make it then.
- No problem.

Changing arrangements:
- I'm afraid I can't come on Friday.
- I'm very busy that day.
- I'm sorry, I can't make it on Tuesday.
- We've got an appointment for ten o'clock, but I'm afraid something's come up.
- Could we fix another time?

Closing:
- Good. So, I'll see you on the 8th.
- Thank you. Goodbye.
- Right. OK, then.

Mini notatka do nauczenia:
- queue = line = kolejka
- motorway = freeway = autostrada
- hand luggage = carry-on baggage = bagaz podreczny
- We are going to meet the client. = Mamy zamiar spotkac sie z klientem.
- I'll call you later. = Zadzwonie pozniej.
- The flight leaves at 9:00. = Lot odlatuje o 9:00.
- Could we meet on Monday? = Czy mozemy spotkac sie w poniedzialek?
