4. Skills - starting and structuring presentations

Introducing yourself:
- On behalf of Alpha Advertising, I'd like to welcome you.
- My name's Marc Hayward.
- Hi, everyone, I'm Marc Hayward.
- Good to see you all.

Introducing the topic:
- I'm going to tell you about the ideas we've come up with for the ad campaign.
- This morning, I'd like to outline the campaign concept we've developed for you.

Giving a plan of your talk:
- I've divided my presentation into three parts.
- Firstly, I'll give you the background.
- Secondly, I'll discuss the media we plan to use.
- Finally, I'll talk you through the storyboard.
- My talk is in three parts.
- I'll start with the background to the campaign, move on to the media we plan to use, and finish with the storyboard for the commercial.

Inviting questions:
- If there's anything you're not clear about, feel free to stop me and ask any questions.
- I'd be grateful if you could leave any questions to the end.

Signposting - jezyk do przechodzenia miedzy czesciami:
- firstly
- secondly
- finally
- on the next slide
- moving on to
- in the final part
- before we go to
- the key points are that ...

- billboard = billboard
