UNIT 2 - TRAVEL

Zakres z ksiazki:
- p.16 Listening: What business travellers want

1. O czym jest listening

W nagraniu Sholto Smith z Hyatt Hotels mowi, czego oczekuja business travellers od hotelu.

Najwazniejsze potrzeby gosci biznesowych:
- good location = dobra lokalizacja
- good transport links = dobre polaczenia komunikacyjne
- close to the airport or client's office = blisko lotniska albo biura klienta
- Internet and technology = internet i zaplecze technologiczne
- business centre = centrum biznesowe
- good restaurant = dobra restauracja

2. How hotels add value

W nagraniu pojawiaja sie dodatkowe udogodnienia dla gosci:
- newspapers on a daily basis = codzienna prasa
- membership to the health club = dostep do klubu fitness / health club
- transportation to and from the airport = transport na i z lotniska
- shuttle service = bus / transfer hotelowy
- shuttle to the airport = transport na lotnisko
- shuttle to the business district or office area = transport do dzielnicy biznesowej / miejsca pracy klienta

3. Przydatne slowa i zwroty

- business traveller = podrozujacy sluzbowo
- meet the needs of guests = odpowiadac na potrzeby gosci
- location = lokalizacja
- underground network = siec metra
- business centre = centrum biznesowe
- non-smoking = dla niepalacych
- adding value = dawanie dodatkowej wartosci
- guest stay = pobyt goscia
- shuttle service = autobus / transfer wahadlowy
- transportation = transport

4. Co warto umiec powiedziec po listeningu

- Business travellers want hotels in convenient locations.
- Fast Internet and a business centre are important.
- Hotels add value by offering transport and other services.
- A good hotel should save the guest time.

5. Typowe pytania egzaminacyjne

- What does Sholto Smith mention as important for business travellers?
- How do hotels add value to a guest's stay?
- What transport services are mentioned?
- What future developments in business travel are expected?
